# Privacy policy

Updated November 8, 2026

ApocalypseGearLab is operated from Quebec, Canada and provides documentary equipment information for Canadian visitors. This page explains the data involved in browsing the site and the choices available to you.

## Your analytics choice

Optional Google Analytics is off by default for all visitors. The Google tag loads only after you select Allow analytics in the analytics preferences or when a valid saved choice can be safely reused. You can use the site and open Amazon.ca links without allowing analytics.

You can change your choice in the footer on any page. Withdrawing permission stops this site’s analytics tracking, removes accessible Google Analytics cookies and reloads the current page to unload the Google tag. It does not erase information already received by Google.

[Open analytics preferences](#analytics-preferences).

Without JavaScript, optional analytics stays off.

## What optional analytics processes

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics helps us understand traffic and interactions. Enhanced measurement is enabled for page views, scrolls, outbound clicks, site search, form interactions, video engagement and file downloads, when supported interactions are present. It may include search terms, link and file details, form identifiers and destination URLs, or video details and progress.

We also send custom equipment-link and documentary-source click events, including the page path, product name and identifier, Canadian ASIN, destination and button placement. We remove query strings and fragments from the page address and referrer supplied by our configuration. This does not remove every automatic event parameter; do not enter personal information in search fields or page URLs.

Email-address redaction is enabled, but it is not a guarantee that all personal information is removed. GA4 user-provided data collection, including automatic detection of email addresses, phone numbers, names and addresses, is disabled. Universal Analytics event collection is disabled. There are no Google Ads links in this GA4 property.

The Google tag also processes browser, device and network information when it communicates with Google. Analytics identifiers distinguish visits and sessions; they do not tell us your name. Advertising storage, Google signals and personalized advertising signals are disabled in this site’s configuration. Google account settings and Google’s processing are separate from this site’s code.

Google Analytics may set first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier. This site configures an expiry of 180 days without extending it on each visit; your browser may limit the duration further. Blocking cookies alone does not necessarily stop analytics requests, which is why we block the tag until you allow analytics.

Google processes analytics data on its infrastructure, which may be outside Quebec or Canada. We do not claim that analytics data is anonymous or stored only in Quebec or Canada.

Event data retention and user data retention in our GA4 property are each set to 2 months. Reset user data on new activity is disabled, so new activity does not renew the user-identifier retention period. These controls apply to covered user-level and event-level data; they do not limit standard aggregated reports. The site’s 180-day cookie and preference durations are separate.

All four optional account data-sharing settings are disabled: Google products & services, Modeling contributions & business insights, Technical support, and Recommendations for your business. Google still processes data to provide, maintain and protect Analytics. These controls are separate from explicitly linked products and Google-tag destinations.

[How Google uses information from sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). [Google Analytics cookie information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207?hl=en-GB).

## Preferences stored on your device

We use local and session storage for your language choice and to keep your place when switching languages. The keys are agl-language and agl-language-position. We store your analytics choice, its version and decision time under agl-analytics-consent; that choice is valid for 180 days and is not an advertising identifier.

A saved grant is reused only when its storage can also record a withdrawal. With unavailable storage, an explicit choice applies only to the current page. We briefly write and remove a fixed storage-check value to verify that a saved grant can be withdrawn; it is not an analytics identifier. You can clear site storage in your browser; clearing a saved choice does not grant permission.

## Hosting and external destinations

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. Page delivery also involves the requested address and browser information. Declining optional analytics does not stop this hosting processing. GitHub handles hosting data under its own privacy practices.

[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Amazon.ca links contain our affiliate tag and the recorded product ASIN. Amazon receives the visit when you choose to open its site and handles data under its own privacy notice. This does not require permission for our optional analytics. Other external sources have their own privacy practices.

[Amazon.ca Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.ca/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ). [Affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Your choices and privacy requests

You can decline or withdraw optional analytics using the footer, block cookies or clear site storage in your browser, and choose whether to visit external sites.

For privacy-related requests about this website’s handling of personal information, contact the Privacy Officer at [apocalypsegearlab@gmail.com](mailto:apocalypsegearlab@gmail.com). This address is reserved for privacy requests, including questions about processing and requests for access, correction or withdrawal where applicable. Please do not use it for customer support, general inquiries or commercial solicitation.

The sole operator serves as Privacy Officer and personally reviews requests for access, correction, withdrawal of consent and deletion, where applicable.

Messages to this address use Google’s Gmail service. We use the information you send to handle and respond to your privacy request. These messages may be processed outside Quebec or Canada. Google processes email under its own [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Describe your privacy request using only the information needed to handle it. Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents or other sensitive information by email. Do not post personal information in public repository issues.
