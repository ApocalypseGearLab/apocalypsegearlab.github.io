# Single-operator privacy-request procedure — proposed for adoption

Version 1, November 8, 2026. **Draft operating procedure; not a claim of existing practice.** The sole operator/Privacy Officer already accepts personal responsibility for applicable requests. The steps below become operating commitments only after owner adoption and necessary account/copy controls are confirmed. No messages were read, sent or deleted during preparation.

## Set up once

Use apocalypsegearlab@gmail.com for privacy requests, not general support. Keep request records out of GitHub/public issues and GA4. Check authorized mailbox access, delegation, forwarding, connected apps and local/download copies; account/device safeguards must fit the risk. Proposed defaults are strong authentication with recovery protection, secured devices and no unnecessary forwarding/delegation. Two-factor authentication is a recommended implementation control, not a claim that the statute names that exact product feature. Record any equivalent control or necessary exception.

Keep a minimal private case register: case ID, received date, request type/scope, identity/authority check method and result (not an ID scan), response due date, action/status, decision reason, closure date and any retention hold/review trigger. A protected local file or adequately controlled existing system is enough; no new paid ticketing service is needed. Do not populate the public template in this repository with real personal information.

## Receive, clarify and verify

1. Read incoming privacy mail often enough to meet applicable deadlines; record receipt rather than waiting for a weekly batch. Separate an access/correction request, analytics-withdrawal question, complaint or deletion request. Acknowledge/clarify using minimal information; no invented acknowledgment SLA is imposed.
2. Confirm control of the relevant existing email thread/address for mailbox records. Do not treat control of any email address as proof of another person’s identity. Ask only for the minimum extra evidence proportionate to the record/risk; verify a representative’s authority where relevant. Do not routinely request passport/driver-licence scans, passwords, card details or copies of an entire identity document. If safer verification is needed, agree a suitable channel before exchanging sensitive material.
3. Help narrow an unclear request. If verification remains inadequate, do not disclose someone else’s data; explain what is needed and issue a legally appropriate written decision rather than silently abandoning the request. Do not presume that clarification or identity checks automatically restart or suspend the legal response clock.

For written access/correction requests, Quebec’s private-sector rules require a prompt written response no later than **30 days from receipt**; this is a sourced legal deadline, not an invented owner service promise. Calendar it immediately. [CAI rights guidance](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/citoyens-protection-renseignements-personnels/acces-et-protection-renseignements-personnels).

## Search and act

| Request | Proposed practical action |
|---|---|
| Access | Search the relevant mailbox/thread, case notes and any identified controlled copies. Describe purposes/categories and provide the person’s own responsive records through a suitable channel, protecting third-party information. If none can be found, state the search scope and limitations rather than inventing a result. |
| Correction | Check the disputed item, update the controlled record where justified, address necessary downstream corrections, and provide the corrected record/appropriate confirmation. Do not rewrite historical correspondence as if it never existed. |
| Consent withdrawal | Explain/use the site’s footer preferences; analytics permission is revoked, accessible cookies cleared and the tag unloaded by reload. No identity proof is needed merely to decline browser analytics. Distinguish future tracking from a separate request about previously collected records. |
| Analytics access/deletion | This site has no name-to-visitor account mapping and user-provided collection is disabled. Do not send a requester’s email/name to GA4 to create a match. If a person can safely establish a relevant existing client identifier, use appropriate authorized provider tools; explain when a record cannot reliably be linked. Do not claim a submission receipt proves completed backend deletion. Google documents [user-deletion functionality](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/config/admin/v1/rest/v1alpha/properties/submitUserDeletion). No deletion API was called here. |
| Deletion of controlled request records | Check applicable grounds/ongoing rights/legal preservation needs; remove unnecessary responsive copies when permitted. State what was removed, what must remain and why. A deletion request is not a promise to erase every provider security log or override legal holds. |
| Complaint | Log the concern, review the facts personally, consider correction/remediation, and answer with reasons and available recourse. As a one-person operation, do not invent an independent internal appeals officer; outside CAI recourse remains available. |

Use written replies for applicable access/correction decisions. If refusing wholly/partly, identify the actual legal basis and relevant review route/time limit; do not use a generic “security reasons” template as a legal conclusion. The CAI explains the [review/complaint routes](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/citoyens-protection-renseignements-personnels/recours-devant-commission), including the usual 30-day disagreement-review period. Internal complaint handling does not remove that external right.

## Retain only for identified purposes, then delete

Proposed rule for owner adoption: keep the minimum material needed to handle the case and any specific applicable preservation/review obligation; once those purposes end, delete unnecessary identifiable copies securely. Review at closure and when any hold ends. Record the reason and review trigger for an exception; do not retain entire inbox threads indefinitely “just in case.” No arbitrary fixed mailbox-retention period is represented as law or current practice. [CAI retention/destruction guidance](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/conservation-destruction-renseignements-personnels).

Where an access/correction refusal engages preservation for legal recourse, retain the relevant information until that requirement ends; see section 36 of the [Act](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/P-39.1). Scope the hold to what is necessary. A concise decision log may have a different justified lifecycle from a sensitive attachment; record the reason instead of silently retaining both forever.

For Gmail, archiving is not deletion. When a deletion is appropriate, locate the relevant individual messages and copies (including sent replies, attachments/downloads and any authorized forwarded/synchronized copies); delete only the intended items and clear their Trash copies as appropriate. Google’s [Gmail deletion documentation](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7401?hl=en) describes Trash recovery/permanent deletion; the provider’s Trash window is not the operator’s retention policy. Do not promise instant removal from provider backups or statutory retention systems.

Before sending a response, recheck the recipient and attachments; avoid copying unrelated people. If a mistaken disclosure/compromise is discovered, stop further exposure, preserve necessary evidence and assess applicable confidentiality-incident duties. A complete incident-management program is outside this narrow request workflow; a serious incident is not handled by simply deleting the email.
