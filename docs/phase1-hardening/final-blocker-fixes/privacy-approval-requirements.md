## Evidence and limits

The business address is absent from every emitted HTML/JS/JSON/XML/text/CSS file in dist; [contact gate evidence](evidence/privacy-contact-gate.json). The documentation drafts themselves contain the address and must remain local until public exposure is approved. noindex is an indexing directive and does not prevent a page or repository document being public.

Google requires policy disclosure of Analytics and its data/cookie processing; see [Analytics terms, section 7](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/us/). Canadian guidance calls for understandable choices and withdrawal while leaving organizations responsible for their context-specific obligations; see [OPC meaningful-consent guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/appropriate-handling-of-personal-information/collecting-personal-information-and-consent/consent/gl_omc_201805/). The Gmail disclosure follows [Google’s policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), which covers sent/received email; actual mailbox settings remain unverified. These primary sources were checked on November 7, 2026. This is a technical review and a proposed policy draft, not legal certification.
