## Basis, scope and proportionality

The operator/location/role and GA4 settings are owner-confirmed. The [fact record](evidence/owner-verified-facts.json) distinguishes the latest confirmations from remaining unknowns; it supersedes the earlier acceptance/BigQuery/access uncertainty without changing historical evidence. Source and passing browser evidence establish static hosting, browser-only preferences, tag gating, native retailer links and the existing catalogue search form. Visitor/request volumes are not measured here; field types and collection paths are known, actual record counts are not invented. There is no site checkout, account-registration system or privacy-upload form in the reviewed implementation. Google and GitHub may independently hold service/security records.

This is a bounded technology-project and outside-Quebec review, not a claim that every external link is an outsourced processor. The conservative scope includes hosting and mailbox service arrangements while distinguishing providers’ own security/account processing. The analysis follows the CAI’s [proportionate EFVP approach](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/uploads/pdfs/CAI_GU_EFVP.pdf) and its [private-enterprise transfer guidance](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/utilisation-communication-renseignements-personnels).

Risk ratings below are qualitative project judgments: low means limited likely harm under the stated controls, medium means meaningful exposure or unresolved control/contract evidence, and high means plausible sensitive disclosure or account compromise requiring mitigation. They are not measured probabilities or a statutory scoring system.

H3 applicable contract/role coverage: the published standard terms are the disclosed working basis, not proof of a private agreement; flag a different enterprise/custom agreement if one changes this flow, and determine DPA scope for any on-behalf processing. **Preliminary disposition:** no evidence currently requires replacing Pages or buying a new plan.
